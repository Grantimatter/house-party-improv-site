Perfect for date night, groups of friends, team building, meetup groups, birthdays, or just treating yourself to a night out.

We are a hidden gem of entertainment in San Antonio, with audience members coming back every single week. Come see why!

FAQ

Common Questions

Where is your theater? ▼
We are located inside Black Potion at 1900 Fredericksburg Rd. Ste 102, San Antonio, TX 78238.
Can I bring alcohol? ▼
No outside alcohol, but we do have a bar with beer and wine for sale. 21+ Please drink responsibly.
What is the minimum age to watch a show? ▼
While there is no set age requirement, please be aware that our shows are made up on the spot and often contain adult content and language. We recommend children under 16 be accompanied by an adult.
How long are the shows? ▼
Shows typically run about 1.5 to 2 hours with an intermission.
Do I need improv experience to take a class? ▼
Not at all! Our Level 1 class is perfect for beginners. We also offer workshops that require no experience. All classes are 18+ unless otherwise noted.
Is there parking? ▼
Yes, there is plenty of parking available at Black Potion's location.
